Questions

How much is it?

Try SpringDesk free for 7 days. After that it's one payment in the Microsoft Store, with every update included and no subscription. The launch price is US$4.99 instead of US$7.99 until January 1, 2027; the Store shows the price in your currency.

Does SpringDesk move my files?

No. Your files stay in your Desktop folder; SpringDesk only shows them in boxes. Uninstall it and your desktop is exactly as it was.

Does it work on Windows 10?

No. SpringDesk is made for Windows 11 and nothing else, so it can look and feel like part of it: it follows Windows 11's light and dark mode and uses Windows' own right-click menu. It doesn't run on Windows 10.

How is it different from Fences?

Fences is the best-known desktop organizer, with more options such as custom sorting rules and colors for each fence, and it runs on Windows 10 too. SpringDesk takes a different approach: clear glass made for Windows 11, new files sorted by type with nothing to set up, Space to look inside files and typing on the desktop to find them, all for one payment that is less than a year of the Fences subscription. See the full comparison.

I use Fences or another desktop organizer. Can I switch?

Yes. Quit the other organizer first (SpringDesk asks you to if Fences is running), then start SpringDesk: it sorts your desktop into boxes on its first run, and you can rearrange everything from there. Layouts from other apps aren't imported.

Does it collect my data?

No. There's no account, no ads and no data collection; everything happens on your PC. Read the privacy policy.

Is SpringDesk actively maintained?

Yes. SpringDesk had more than a dozen updates in its first week in the Microsoft Store (from October 17, 2026), most of them fixes and improvements people asked for. Every change is listed in the changelog, and updates install automatically through the Store.

Is it in my language?

SpringDesk is in English and Chinese, and follows your Windows display language.

Something isn't working.

Open Settings › About › Export diagnostics and open an issue with the zip, or email it to contact@springdesk.app. It holds logs and your layout, with file names but no file contents.
