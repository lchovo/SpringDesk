Changelog

Every SpringDesk update, newest first. Updates are free and arrive automatically through the Microsoft Store. Found a bug? Email contact@springdesk.app or open an issue. Every report is read, and fixes ship as Store updates.

Latest version: 1.15.3, 2026-11-25

1.15.3 · November 25, 2026

  • New: Settings › About has links to the website, the changelog and the privacy policy, plus our email address. Click the address to write to us, or copy it.
  • Improved: new folder boxes open subfolders right in the box. To open them in File Explorer instead, right-click the box’s title and turn off Open subfolders in the box. Folder boxes you already have stay as they are.

1.15.1 · November 24, 2026

  • New: SpringDesk speaks ten languages: English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Português (Brasil), 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文 and 繁體中文. It follows your Windows display language, or you can pick another one in Settings › General › Language. Names SpringDesk gave your boxes and tabs (Inbox, Favorites, Tab 2…) change with it; names you typed stay as they are.
  • Improved: search ignores accents, so typing “resume” finds “Résumé”.
  • Fixed: boxes could flash when you used Show desktop with windows open.

1.14.1 · November 23, 2026

  • Fixed: a brief flash when you clicked the desktop after using Show desktop.

1.14.0 · November 23, 2026

  • New: folder boxes can open subfolders right in the box, like File Explorer. Right-click the box’s title and choose Open subfolders in the box (off by default, set for each box). Double-click a subfolder to see its files; the arrow at the top left, Backspace, Alt+Left arrow or your mouse’s back button take you back up, as far as the box’s own folder.

1.13.2 · November 23, 2026

  • New: reinstalled SpringDesk? It offers to bring your boxes back from a copy of your layout kept in Documents › SpringDesk.
  • Improved: Sort by in a box works like the Windows desktop and adds Size. Each choice sorts once; choose the same one again right away to reverse the order.
  • Improved: Recently opened is now a switch under Sort by (right-click an empty spot in a box › Sort by › Recently opened). It’s off after this update, so turn it on in the boxes where you want it.
  • Improved: the desktop’s right-click menu is shorter. Paste appears only when there’s something to paste, and Use SpringDesk and Settings are in SpringDesk’s icon in the notification area.
  • Improved: new files on the desktop always land at the front of their box.
  • Improved: if SpringDesk closes unexpectedly, it opens again by itself.
  • Improved: better stability and smoother performance.
  • Fixed: Show desktop could stop working while some full-screen games were running.
  • Fixed: some pop-up windows could end up hidden behind your boxes.
  • Fixed: boxes could move to another screen after you reconnected a dock or display.
  • Fixed: files could lose their places in your boxes when the desktop folder was slow to connect at sign-in.

1.13.1 · November 20, 2026

  • Fixed: on some PCs with certain right-click menu add-ons, SpringDesk could close by itself shortly after starting.

1.13.0 · November 20, 2026

  • New: hide boxes when you’re away. Turn it on in Settings › Boxes and choose how long it waits, from 10 seconds to 10 minutes. Your boxes fade out to leave just the wallpaper and come back when you move the mouse, press a key or unlock the PC.
  • New: box settings have their own page, Settings › Boxes. Turn Auto-sort on or off there, and choose in File types which kinds of files get a tab in the sorting box. Turning a kind off closes its tab and moves its files back to the Inbox.
  • Improved: Settings are shorter and plainer, with explanations behind a small ? next to a setting’s name.
  • Improved: better visuals for the glass after you move a box to another screen and with live wallpapers.
  • Fixed: after you changed resolution or scaling, or connected from another device, boxes could fall out of line.

1.12.3 · November 19, 2026

  • Improved: smoother resizing of boxes, with better visuals for the glass edge.

1.12.2 · November 19, 2026

  • Improved: better visuals for the glass while you drag a box.

1.12.1 · November 19, 2026

  • New: Refresh is back on the desktop’s right-click menu, below View, and F5 works on the desktop and in a box. It reloads the icons and brings in files that hadn’t shown up.
  • Fixed: names could look blurry after you brought back boxes hidden with a double-click.

1.12.0 · November 18, 2026

  • New: you can use Windows’ own menu when you right-click the empty desktop: turn off Use SpringDesk’s desktop menu in Settings › General › Desktop. Add box, Roll up all boxes and Lock boxes then move to the menu of SpringDesk’s icon in the notification area.
  • Improved: when you drag files over a box, a thin outline around it shows where they’ll go.
  • Improved: smoother dragging of boxes from one monitor to another.
  • Fixed: Windows’ own desktop icons you moved while SpringDesk wasn’t running could jump back the next time you quit SpringDesk.
  • Fixed: all your boxes could disappear after you switched displays or connected remotely.

1.11.0 · November 17, 2026

  • New: search inside a box. Click the search button next to the roll-up button and type to filter the current tab. The button appears when the box is wide enough.
  • New: folder boxes have Change folder… in their title and tab menus. A name that came from the folder follows the new one; a name you chose is kept.
  • New: double-click an empty part of a box’s title bar to roll it up or down.
  • New: sort ascending or descending, remembered for each tab.
  • New: video previews have a mute button. Videos start muted the first time, then follow your choice.
  • Improved: each tab remembers where you scrolled.
  • Improved: a new box is ready to name as soon as it appears.

1.10.1 · November 16, 2026

  • Improved: files the preview can’t play show their details straight away, and tabs show their icons at once after you change the display scaling.
  • Fixed: some videos recorded on phones didn’t play in the preview or showed sideways.

1.10.0 · November 16, 2026

  • New: one layout on every screen. When you plug in a monitor, change the scaling or connect remotely, your boxes keep their arrangement and fit the screen. Back on your usual screen, every box is where you left it, along with any changes you made elsewhere.
  • Improved: the layout on screen when this update arrives becomes your layout everywhere; the one from before is kept in Settings › General › Restore an earlier layout.
  • Fixed: after you unlocked the PC or changed displays, boxes could stop responding or disappear until SpringDesk was restarted.

1.9.1 · November 16, 2026

  • Fixed: right-clicking the empty desktop while a box’s menu was still open showed Windows’ menu instead of SpringDesk’s.
  • Fixed: after starting some full-screen games or connecting remotely, boxes could stop responding until SpringDesk was restarted.

1.9.0 · November 14, 2026

  • New: right-clicking the empty desktop opens SpringDesk’s own menu: View, New, Paste, Add box, Roll up all boxes, Lock boxes, Display settings, Personalize, Next desktop background, Use SpringDesk and Settings. A new box appears where you clicked, and what other apps add to the menu is under Show more options.
  • Fixed: with Windows Spotlight as your wallpaper, the edges of your boxes could keep showing the previous picture after Next desktop background.

1.8.0 · November 13, 2026

  • New: icons in boxes come in small, medium and large, as on the Windows desktop. Pick one in Settings › Appearance › Icon size, or right-click an empty spot in a box › View. Your boxes keep their size and place.
  • Fixed: the menu of SpringDesk’s icon in the notification area didn’t follow a switch between light and dark.
  • Fixed: a change to your boxes made just before shutting down or signing out could be lost.

1.7.2 · November 7, 2026

  • Improved: SpringDesk appears within seconds of signing in.

1.7.1 · November 5, 2026

  • Improved: a calmer Settings window, with smaller switches, softer motion and a mark showing which page you’re on.
  • Fixed: with a live wallpaper, boxes could turn grey while you dragged one.
  • Fixed: SpringDesk’s icon, once moved from the hidden icons onto the taskbar, went back after each update. Move it out once more after this update and it stays.

1.7.0 · November 5, 2026

  • New: double-click an empty spot on the desktop to hide all your boxes and see just the wallpaper; double-click again to bring them back. You can turn this off in Settings › General.
  • New: change the shortcuts: in Settings › General, click the shortcut under Roll all boxes up and down or Search from anywhere, then press the new one.
  • Improved: smoother dragging between screens with different scaling, and better performance overall.
  • Improved: the Transparency slider in Settings › Appearance follows the mouse smoothly.
  • Fixed: with a live wallpaper, the glass could show the regular wallpaper instead.

1.6.0 · November 5, 2026

  • Improved: Search from anywhere opens with Alt+Space (it was Ctrl+Alt+Space). If another app already uses Alt+Space, SpringDesk keeps Ctrl+Alt+Space and Settings says so.
  • Improved: rolling all boxes up or down (Ctrl+Alt+H or the notification-area menu) moves every box together, more smoothly.
  • Improved: smoother dragging of boxes on high-resolution screens and with live wallpapers, and better performance overall.

1.5.10 · November 4, 2026

  • Improved: much smoother dragging of boxes, above all with live wallpapers and on high-resolution screens.
  • Improved: in a narrow box with several tabs, tab names show as much as fits, and pointing at one shows its whole name.
  • Improved: with a preview open, clicking a file in another box shows that file.
  • Improved: your layout is safe even if the power goes off while it’s being saved, and restoring an older layout keeps a copy of the current one first.
  • Improved: a folder tab on a network drive or USB drive comes back by itself when the drive is back.
  • Fixed: smaller issues with right-click menus, folder tabs and renaming.

1.5.9 · November 4, 2026

  • Fixed: long names of selected icons could be cut off on some displays.

1.5.8 · November 3, 2026

  • Improved: during the free trial, after you’ve used SpringDesk for a few days, it asks once whether you’d like to rate it in the Microsoft Store.

1.5.7 · October 30, 2026

  • New: search results work like icons: click to select, double-click to open, drag one out into a chat, a folder or another box, right-click for its menu, Ctrl+C to copy, Ctrl+Enter to show it in its folder.
  • New: Ctrl+Alt+H rolls all boxes up (before you share your screen, say), and pressing it again brings them back. You can turn it off in Settings › General.
  • Improved: selecting an icon with a long name shows its whole name, as on the Windows desktop.
  • Improved: the glass catches up with the desktop at once after the PC wakes up or is unlocked.
  • Fixed: a program opened from a box could appear behind your boxes.
  • Fixed: with a live wallpaper, boxes could sometimes turn grey.

1.5.6 · October 25, 2026

  • Improved: files that are still downloading show up in their box within a second.
  • Improved: scroll bars are easier to grab, and clicking above or below the bar scrolls a page, as in Windows.
  • Improved: in Settings and dialogs, the Tab key shows which button or switch you’re on.
  • Improved: in a box, Shift+F10 or the Menu key opens the menu of the selected icons, or the box’s menu.
  • Improved: better stability and smoother performance.
  • Fixed: after cut, choosing Skip or Cancel in “Replace or Skip Files” could leave the rest of what you cut impossible to paste.
  • Fixed: double-clicking a box’s roll-up arrow could leave the box open with no icons showing.

1.5.5 · October 23, 2026

  • New: Roll up all boxes (or Unroll all boxes) is in the menu of SpringDesk’s icon in the notification area, and new boxes are made from there too.
  • Improved: SpringDesk’s menus are in clear groups, with Open in File Explorer at the top of a box’s menus.
  • Improved: a box you drag stands out with frosted glass and an accent-colored ring until it settles into place.
  • Fixed: smaller visual issues when dragging boxes.

1.5.4 · October 23, 2026

  • Fixed: programs opened from a box could close together with SpringDesk. They now run on their own, as when opened from the Windows desktop.

1.5.3 · October 22, 2026

  • Improved: easier selection: press anywhere in a box outside an icon’s picture and name to drag a selection box.
  • Improved: boxes keep the same distance from the screen’s edges as from each other.
  • Improved: the Recycle Bin’s icon in a box shows when it’s empty or full, as on the Windows desktop.
  • Improved: files dragged from a folder box onto the empty desktop are moved or copied as Windows would.
  • Improved: better visuals for the glass, and better performance while you’re not using your boxes.
  • Fixed: right-clicking an icon sometimes showed no menu on PCs with certain right-click menu add-ons.
  • Fixed: smaller issues with scrolling in folder boxes, renaming and icons that didn’t appear.

1.5.2 · October 21, 2026

  • Improved: box titles and icon names keep a steady color, readable over light and busy wallpapers.
  • Improved: better visuals for the glass with live wallpapers and when rolling up boxes, and better performance.

1.5.1 · October 21, 2026

  • Improved: better performance with live wallpapers.
  • Fixed: with a live wallpaper on two screens, boxes could sometimes turn grey.

1.5.0 · October 20, 2026

  • New: boxes are clear glass. At the default 100% transparency only the edge shows, catching the light; pointing at a box lights up its rim.
  • New: a rolled-up box is just its name on the desktop; point at it to see its glass strip.
  • New: one Transparency slider in Settings › Appearance replaces Glass and Opacity. Lower it to frost the glass and make text easier to read on a busy wallpaper. Your current look is kept.
  • Improved: names are white with a soft dark halo, as on the Windows desktop, so they stay readable on any wallpaper.

1.4.9 · October 20, 2026

  • Improved: “Show desktop icons” in the desktop’s View menu works as before 1.4.8: turning it off hides the boxes too.
  • Improved: something made with New on the desktop opens ready to rename; files saved or downloaded to the desktop no longer do.
  • Fixed: on some PCs, Windows’ own desktop icons could flicker behind the boxes. SpringDesk no longer changes your “Show desktop icons” setting and puts back any change 1.4.8 made.
  • Fixed: files dragged from another computer or a remote session couldn’t be dropped on the empty desktop.

1.4.8 · October 20, 2026

  • New: something you make on the desktop (New, Ctrl+Shift+N) opens in its box with its name ready to type.
  • New: ticking Windows’ “Show desktop icons” switches SpringDesk off; unticking it switches SpringDesk on.
  • Improved: switching SpringDesk off or quitting puts Windows’ desktop icons back exactly where they were.
  • Improved: one selection on the whole desktop, as in Windows; icons follow when you change which program opens a file type; right-click menus appear the moment you release the button.
  • Improved: better stability, and a smaller download.

1.4.7 · October 19, 2026

  • New: one switch, Use SpringDesk, in Settings › General › Desktop and in the notification-area menu. Turn it off to get Windows’ own desktop back without quitting; your boxes and layout are kept for when you turn it on again.
  • Fixed: a document opened from a box could stay behind other windows in some apps.

1.4.6 · October 19, 2026

  • New: in a box, Shift+arrow keys select several icons, Ctrl+Shift+N makes a folder and Alt+Enter shows Properties.
  • New: during the free trial, Settings › About shows the days left and a Buy button.
  • Improved: dragging files into and out of boxes follows Windows: within a drive they move; to another drive, a USB drive or a network folder they’re copied. Ctrl copies, Shift moves.
  • Improved: search finds Chinese names by full pinyin, and keeps what you typed if you switch to another window.
  • Improved: files that are still downloading wait quietly in the Inbox.
  • Fixed: on some PCs, This PC never appeared in a box. SpringDesk now shows the same system icons as the Windows desktop.
  • Fixed: after a click on the empty desktop, Ctrl+A, Delete or Enter could act on Windows’ hidden desktop icons.

1.4.5 · October 19, 2026

  • New: Ctrl+Z in a box undoes your last file operation (move, rename, delete…), as on the desktop.
  • Improved: files dropped into a box from other programs land the way they would on the Windows desktop and stay in that box.
  • Improved: after renaming, the next key works in the box.
  • Improved: if SpringDesk ever restores your layout from a backup, it tells you which day’s.
  • Fixed: on some PCs your boxes went back to an older layout after a restart or an update. They’re saved now, and the first start of this version brings back your boxes as you last left them.

1.4.4 · October 19, 2026

  • Improved: a file in the sorting box renamed to another type moves to the tab for its new type.
  • Improved: a file dragged out of a box onto the empty desktop flies into the Inbox, which flashes so you can see where it went.
  • Improved: renaming shows the whole name, however long, as on the Windows desktop.
  • Improved: if your layout file is ever damaged, your boxes come back from the latest daily backup.
  • Improved: quitting SpringDesk brings Windows’ desktop icons back first, so the desktop is never empty.
  • Improved: diagnostics you export leave out your user name and other programs’ window titles.
  • Fixed: New on the empty desktop’s right-click menu could show an error instead of making the file.
  • Fixed: new documents made with New didn’t always go to their tab in the sorting box.
  • Fixed: smaller issues with renaming, downloads and previews.

1.4.3 · October 18, 2026

  • New: after you’ve used SpringDesk for a few days, it asks once whether you’d like to rate it in the Microsoft Store. You can also rate it any time in Settings › About.

1.4.2 · October 18, 2026

  • Improved: copy a file in a box and paste it into the same tab to get “name - Copy”, as in File Explorer; while renaming, dragging across the name selects text.

1.4.1 · October 18, 2026

  • New: frosted glass (Settings › Appearance › Glass): the wallpaper behind a box melts into soft, bright color, so a busy wallpaper no longer shows through. It works with live wallpapers too.
  • New: resize boxes from their corners, not just their edges, and make them as small as 2 icons wide and 1 row tall.

1.4.0 · October 17, 2026

  • New: first release in the Microsoft Store, with a 7-day free trial.

Before the Microsoft Store

Versions 1.0 to 1.3 (October 16–17, 2026) were early free builds on GitHub. They are no longer available; the Store version replaced them and takes over their layouts.

1.3.2 · October 17, 2026

  • Fixed: photo thumbnails could appear upside down, and a name that just fit on one line could lose its last letter.

1.3.1 · October 17, 2026

  • Improved: better performance when previewing pictures.
  • Fixed: some text files showed garbled characters in the preview.
  • Fixed: with several icons selected, the arrow keys in the preview went beyond the selection.
  • Fixed: a picture edited on the desktop kept its old thumbnail, and a folder box sorted by date didn’t move a changed file to the top.
  • Fixed: restoring an earlier layout could also reset your theme and settings.
  • Fixed: new desktop files could go missing when there was no Inbox.

1.3.0 · October 17, 2026

  • New: select an icon and press Space to look inside without opening it; Space or Esc closes it. Pictures, videos, sound, text, code and PDFs show right there; documents, spreadsheets, presentations, zip files, programs and folders show a card with their details.
  • New: in the preview, the arrow keys go to the next item, the mouse wheel zooms pictures, videos play straight away, and Enter opens the file.
  • New: in a box, the arrow keys move the selection, as on the desktop.
  • New: light and dark: choose Light, Dark or Auto in Settings › Appearance › Theme. Auto follows Windows.
  • Improved: Settings › General › Desktop has “Show desktop icons” and “Also show Windows’ own icons” side by side.
  • Improved: Settings opens from the notification-area icon instead of each box’s menu.
  • Fixed: names in desktop search results were hard to read on the dark background.

1.2.0 · October 17, 2026

  • New: SpringDesk in English as well as Chinese. It follows your Windows display language, or you can choose in Settings › General › Language.
  • New: names SpringDesk gave your boxes and tabs (Inbox, Favorites, Tab 2…) change with the language; names you typed stay as they are.
  • New: Settings, rebuilt: four pages (General, Sorting, Appearance, About), with version and diagnostics under About.
  • New: SpringDesk asks and tells in its own frosted dialogs, with buttons that say what they do (“Delete”, “Restore”).
  • Improved: rename, lock and delete a box from its right-click menu; Restore an earlier layout is in Settings › General.
  • Improved: your layout is backed up once a day, before the day’s first change, so you can always go back to how it looked before today.
  • Improved: shorter, clearer wording throughout, and a Sort by menu in boxes.

1.1.0 · October 17, 2026

  • New: the “Files” box is now a sorting box: one tab per kind of file (documents, spreadsheets, presentations, PDF, pictures, and if you like videos, archives, installers, web links and system icons). Your files stay where they were.
  • New: turn the sorting box’s tabs and their auto-sorting on and off from its title bar › Tabs, or on the Sorting page in the control center. Closing a tab sends its files back to the Inbox.
  • New: drag from an empty spot in a box to select several icons, as on the Windows desktop; hold Ctrl to add or remove.
  • New: Open in Explorer in a folder box’s tab and title menus.
  • Improved: a sorting box tab only takes its own kind of file.
  • Improved: new program shortcuts and folders wait in the Inbox.
  • Improved: copy, cut and paste between boxes work like File Explorer, including “name - Copy” when pasting into the same folder.
  • Improved: Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X and Delete respond at once, and better performance overall.
  • Fixed: a file you made in a box with New could jump to another tab.
  • Fixed: saving a document could move it out of the box you put it in.
  • Fixed: keyboard shortcuts in a box worked only right after clicking an icon.
  • Fixed: smaller issues with dragging icons and with Settings.

1.0.0 · October 16, 2026

  • New: first release, as a free build on GitHub. Formerly DeskFlow, now SpringDesk. Requires Windows 11.
  • New: create, rename, move and resize boxes, and roll a box up to its title bar.
  • New: tabs: several groups of icons in one box; drop a box onto another box’s title bar to merge them.
  • New: folder boxes: a box that shows a folder, such as Downloads.
  • New: your desktop sorted on first use into programs, files, folders and downloads, with anything unclear in the Inbox; new files on the desktop find their place by type.
  • New: boxes snap to a grid and never overlap; lock the layout against accidental drags.
  • New: recently opened icons move to the front, and pointing at an icon shows the same details as Windows.
  • New: click the desktop or a box and just type to search, including Chinese names by pinyin initials; or press Ctrl+Alt+Space anytime.
  • New: a control center for appearance, lock and layout backups.
  • New: an installer; SpringDesk starts with Windows, and updates arrive with one click to restart.
  • New: export diagnostics from the control center › About to send when something goes wrong.
  • New: upgrading from DeskFlow keeps your boxes.
