Changelog

Every SpringDesk update, newest first. Updates are free and arrive automatically through the Microsoft Store. Found a bug? Email contact@springdesk.app or open an issue. Every report is read, and fixes ship as Store updates.

Latest version: 1.15.1, 2026-11-24

1.15.1 · November 24, 2026

  • New: SpringDesk speaks ten languages: English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Português (Brasil), 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文 and 繁體中文. It follows your Windows display language, or you can pick another one in Settings › General › Language. Default names SpringDesk gave your boxes and tabs (Inbox, Favorites, Tab 2…) change with it, while names you typed stay as they are.
  • Improved: search ignores accents, so typing “resume” finds “Résumé”.
  • Fixed: boxes could flash briefly when you used Show desktop (Win+D or the far right corner of the taskbar) with windows open. They now stay steady.

1.14.1 · November 23, 2026

  • Fixed: wallpaper could flash briefly over your boxes when you clicked an empty part of the desktop to close its right-click menu after using Show desktop. They now stay visible.

1.14.0 · November 23, 2026

  • New: folder boxes can open subfolders right inside the box, like File Explorer. Right-click a box’s title and choose Open subfolders in the box (off by default, set per box). Double-click a subfolder to view its files, and go back up using the top-left arrow, Backspace, Alt+Left arrow or your mouse’s back button, stopping at the box’s own folder.

1.13.2 · November 23, 2026

  • New: restore your boxes after reinstalling SpringDesk. SpringDesk keeps a copy of your layout in Documents › SpringDesk and offers to bring your boxes back.
  • Improved: Sort by in a box now works like the Windows desktop and adds Size. Choosing Name, Size, Type or Date modified sorts once, and choosing the same option again right away flips the order.
  • Improved: Recently opened is now a switch under Sort by (right-click an empty spot in a box › Sort by › Recently opened). It starts turned off after this update, so turn it on in any box where you want it.
  • Improved: the desktop’s right-click menu is shorter. Paste only appears when there is something to paste, while Use SpringDesk and Settings are in SpringDesk’s icon in the notification area.
  • Improved: new files on the desktop always land at the front of their box.
  • Improved: if SpringDesk closes unexpectedly or is closed by an installer, it opens again by itself, and if it stops responding you can open it again from the Start menu to wait or restart it.
  • Improved: the desktop stays responsive when SpringDesk is busy, the first box drag no longer stutters, and quitting no longer gets stuck.
  • Fixed: Show desktop (Win+D) did not respond while a game ran in borderless full screen. It now works again.
  • Fixed: pop-ups such as WeGame’s after-game stats could end up hidden behind your boxes. They now appear on top.
  • Fixed: boxes could move off their screen after you reconnected a dock or when a virtual display came and went. They now stay on their own screen.
  • Fixed: files could lose their places in your boxes if the desktop folder connected late. They now keep their places.

1.13.1 · November 20, 2026

  • Fixed: on PCs with certain right-click menu add-ons, such as Locale Emulator, SpringDesk could appear stuck and close by itself a few seconds after starting. SpringDesk now stays open, and those add-ons work in its right-click menus as they do in File Explorer.

1.13.0 · November 20, 2026

  • New: hide boxes when you’re away. Turn it on in Settings › Boxes and choose a wait time from 10 seconds to 10 minutes. When nobody uses the PC for that long, your boxes fade out to leave only the wallpaper, and they return when you move the mouse, press a key or unlock the PC.
  • New: box settings now have their own page in Settings › Boxes. Use the Auto-sort switch and File types list to pick which kinds of files get tabs in the sorting box, or toggle them from the tab menu on the desktop; turning a type off closes its tab and moves its files back to the Inbox.
  • Improved: Settings text is shorter and plainer, with explanations tucked behind a small ? icon next to each setting’s name.
  • Fixed: switching resolution or scaling, or connecting from another device, could push a box toward the middle of the screen or leave boxes narrower with gaps between them. Your boxes now stay aligned.
  • Fixed: dragging a box to another screen could leave a strip of the old screen’s wallpaper in its glass, or leave the glass out of date if you hid boxes while the wallpaper was updating. The glass now updates cleanly.
  • Fixed: with a live wallpaper such as Wallpaper Engine, dragging a box could stop showing it through frosted glass after Windows Explorer or the wallpaper app restarted. It now keeps showing through.

1.12.3 · November 19, 2026

  • Improved: resizing a box draws its glass edge faster and uses less memory, and tabs with many files use less memory when arranging icons.
  • Fixed: resizing a box from its left or top edge, or letting it snap to the grid, could slide the wallpaper in its glass edge out of line with the middle. It now stays aligned.

1.12.2 · November 19, 2026

  • Fixed: while dragging a box, a ring of sharp wallpaper could show just inside the border around the frosted middle. The glass now stays frosted all the way to the border.

1.12.1 · November 19, 2026

  • New: Refresh is back on the desktop’s right-click menu below View, and F5 works on the desktop and inside boxes. It blinks icons like the Windows desktop, brings in missed files, reloads icons and wallpaper glass, and restarts SpringDesk if a box’s picture has stopped updating.
  • Fixed: names that changed while boxes were hidden by a double-click could look slightly blurry when the boxes returned. They now stay sharp.

1.12.0 · November 18, 2026

  • New: you can switch back to Windows’ own menu when right-clicking the empty desktop by turning off Use SpringDesk’s desktop menu in Settings › General › Desktop. When it is off, Add box, Roll up all boxes and Lock boxes move to SpringDesk’s notification-area icon menu.
  • Improved: dragging files over a box highlights its edge with a thin outline, while the dark blue title bar is used only for merging boxes and tabs.
  • Fixed: if you moved Windows’ own desktop icons while SpringDesk wasn’t running, they could jump back to their old places the next time you quit SpringDesk. They now stay where you put them.
  • Fixed: dragging a box from one monitor onto another could stutter badly as its edge crossed over. The drag no longer stutters.
  • Fixed: with a live wallpaper, box glass could flash right after you double-clicked the desktop to bring boxes back. It now reappears cleanly.
  • Fixed: all boxes could disappear after switching displays or connecting remotely. SpringDesk now brings them back by itself.

1.11.0 · November 17, 2026

  • New: search inside a box. Click the search button beside the roll-up button (shown when the box is wide enough) and type to filter the current tab; clear the text, close search or roll up the box to show every item again.
  • New: folder boxes now have Change folder… in their header and tab menus. Default names follow the new folder, while names you chose are kept.
  • New: double-click an empty part of a box’s header to roll it up or unroll it, while double-clicking its name or a tab still renames it.
  • New: sorting now includes ascending and descending order, remembered separately for each tab.
  • New: video previews now include a mute button. Videos start muted the first time, and your choice is kept afterward.
  • Improved: each tab remembers its scroll position, so switching back shows the same place straight away.
  • Improved: a new box is ready to name as soon as it appears.

1.10.1 · November 16, 2026

  • Fixed: videos from a recent iPhone could drop back to file details in preview (Space), upright phone videos appeared sideways, and videos with “#” in their name failed to play. They now play, upright.
  • Fixed: opening a file that preview cannot play could flicker before showing its details. It now shows its details straight away.
  • Fixed: switching tabs in a box could take a few seconds to show icons after display scaling changed. Icons now appear at once.

1.10.0 · November 16, 2026

  • New: one layout on every screen. When you plug in a monitor, change scaling or connect remotely, your boxes keep their arrangement and scale with the screen instead of using separate layouts per resolution; back on your usual screen, every box returns to its place, while boxes you moved, resized or added stay updated.
  • Improved: your current on-screen layout becomes your shared layout after updating, and your previous layout is kept as a backup in Settings › General › Restore an earlier layout.
  • Fixed: after unlocking the PC or changing displays, boxes could freeze or vanish while right-clicks still opened their menus. SpringDesk now recovers automatically and brings your boxes back as they were.

1.9.1 · November 16, 2026

  • Fixed: right-clicking the empty desktop while a box or file menu was still open brought up Windows’ menu instead of SpringDesk’s. That menu now closes and SpringDesk’s desktop menu opens.
  • Fixed: after starting some games or game launchers, or connecting remotely, boxes could look frozen with high CPU and memory use until SpringDesk was restarted. SpringDesk now detects and recovers stuck boxes within seconds.

1.9.0 · November 14, 2026

  • New: right-clicking the empty desktop now opens SpringDesk’s own menu with View, New, Paste, Add box, Roll up all boxes, Lock boxes, Display settings, Personalize, Next desktop background, Use SpringDesk and Settings. Items added by other apps sit under Show more options, and Add box places the new box where you clicked (moving Add box, Roll up all boxes and Lock boxes from the notification-area icon’s menu).
  • Fixed: with Windows Spotlight as your wallpaper, choosing Next desktop background could leave box edges showing the old wallpaper for up to a minute. The edges now update along with the wallpaper.

1.8.0 · November 13, 2026

  • New: icons in boxes now come in small, medium and large sizes, the same as on the Windows desktop. Pick one in Settings › Appearance › Icon size or right-click an empty space in a box › View; your boxes stay the same size and in the same place.
  • Fixed: switching between light and dark in Settings left the notification-area icon’s menu in its old colors, making hovered items hard to read on a light menu. It now follows your theme.
  • Fixed: changes made to your boxes in the last second before shutting down or signing out could be lost. They are now saved.

1.7.2 · November 7, 2026

  • Improved: SpringDesk now opens within seconds of signing in instead of waiting behind your other startup programs.

1.7.1 · November 5, 2026

  • Improved: toggle switches in Settings are smaller with a softer motion, and they stay still instead of sliding from off to on each time the window opens.
  • Improved: Settings pages fade in as you switch, and a small mark shows which page you are on.
  • Improved: scrollbars in Settings appear only while you scroll.
  • Fixed: with a live wallpaper such as Wallpaper Engine, boxes could cloud over and turn flat grey when dragged. The live wallpaper now shows through the glass again.
  • Fixed: moving SpringDesk’s icon from hidden icons onto the taskbar did not survive updates. It now stays there through future updates (move it out once more after this update).

1.7.0 · November 5, 2026

  • New: double-click an empty spot on the desktop to hide all your boxes and see only the wallpaper, then double-click again to bring them back. You can turn this off in Settings › General.
  • New: customize your shortcuts in Settings › General by clicking the shortcut under Roll all boxes up and down or Search from anywhere, then pressing your new key combination.
  • Improved: SpringDesk uses less memory, gives memory back right away when switched off or after use, and keeps memory steady when dragging boxes across screens.
  • Improved: the Transparency slider in Settings › Appearance follows the mouse smoothly.
  • Fixed: dragging a box between two screens with different scaling could stutter halfway across. It now keeps its size while moving and takes the new screen’s size once dropped.
  • Fixed: with a live wallpaper, box glass could show the static Windows wallpaper instead of the live one. It now shows the live wallpaper.

1.6.0 · November 5, 2026

  • Improved: Search from anywhere now opens with Alt+Space (previously Ctrl+Alt+Space). If another app already uses Alt+Space, SpringDesk uses Ctrl+Alt+Space and shows this in Settings.
  • Improved: rolling all boxes up or down (Ctrl+Alt+H or the tray menu) now moves every box together in sync and more smoothly.
  • Improved: SpringDesk uses less memory when rolling boxes up and down and returns unused memory to Windows while you are away from the keyboard.
  • Fixed: dragging a box could hitch on high-resolution screens or with a live wallpaper. Dragging no longer hitches.

1.5.10 · November 4, 2026

  • Improved: dragging a box is much smoother on high-resolution screens and with a live wallpaper, and SpringDesk uses far less memory with live wallpapers.
  • Improved: in narrow boxes with several tabs, tab names show as much as fits and reveal the full name on hover.
  • Improved: with a preview open, clicking a file in another box shows that file instead of closing the preview.
  • Improved: saving your layout survives a power cut, so your boxes are kept if the PC switches off mid-save.
  • Improved: restoring an older layout backs up your current one first.
  • Improved: a folder tab on a network drive or USB disk comes back by itself when the drive reconnects.
  • Improved: if another app had the search shortcut at start, it starts working once that app releases it.
  • Fixed: smaller issues with right-clicking and releasing outside an icon, two folder tabs holding files with the same name, moving a newly opened file between boxes, renaming newly created files, and the no-room message when adding a box.

1.5.9 · November 4, 2026

  • Fixed: on some screens (such as 4K at 175%) and in narrow boxes, long names on selected icons could be cut off after two lines or clipped on the bottom row. The full name now always shows.

1.5.8 · November 3, 2026

  • Improved: during the free trial, after a few days of use, SpringDesk now asks once whether you would like to rate it in the Microsoft Store rather than waiting until purchase.

1.5.7 · October 30, 2026

  • New: search results work like desktop icons. Click to select, double-click to open, drag out into a chat, folder or another box, right-click for its menu, press Ctrl+C to copy, or press Ctrl+Enter to show it in its folder.
  • New: press Ctrl+Alt+H to roll all boxes up before sharing your screen, and press it again to bring them back. You can turn this off in Settings › General.
  • Improved: selecting an icon with a long name now shows the full name, as on the Windows desktop.
  • Improved: after waking, unlocking or turning the screen back on, box glass catches up with the desktop at once. If Windows stops showing your wallpaper (a black desktop around the boxes), SpringDesk gets the wallpaper back.
  • Fixed: app windows opened from a box or reopened after an update could appear underneath your boxes. Windows now always come up in front.
  • Fixed: with a live wallpaper, boxes could occasionally turn into plain grey panels. They now keep their glass look with the wallpaper showing through.

1.5.6 · October 25, 2026

  • Improved: downloads in progress appear in their box within a second.
  • Improved: scroll bars are easier to catch, and clicking above or below the bar scrolls a page as in Windows.
  • Improved: moving through Settings and dialogs with Tab highlights the button or switch in focus.
  • Improved: pressing Shift+F10 or the Menu key inside a box opens the menu for selected icons, or the box’s menu if nothing is selected.
  • Improved: SpringDesk keeps responding even when File Explorer is busy or stuck.
  • Improved: smoother performance and lower memory use.
  • Fixed: items cut with Ctrl+X could be lost from the clipboard if you clicked Skip or Cancel at “Replace or Skip Files”. You can now still paste them elsewhere until all items are moved.
  • Fixed: double-clicking a box’s roll-up arrow could leave the box open with no icons showing, and dragging a tab then clicking another could highlight the wrong tab.

1.5.5 · October 23, 2026

  • New: the notification-area icon’s menu adds Roll up all boxes (or Unroll all boxes), and new boxes are now created from this menu rather than from individual box menus.
  • Improved: the notification-area icon’s menu is organized into groups for making boxes, all boxes at once, SpringDesk itself, and quitting.
  • Improved: box right-click menus (title, tabs and empty space) are organized into groups with Open in File Explorer always at the top.
  • Improved: dragged boxes frost over with an accent border and sit over other boxes until dropped, fading over another box’s title so you can see it light up to merge.
  • Fixed: dragging a box could leave an extra line inside its edge until the pointer left, and dragging a locked, rolled-up box squeezed its “Boxes are locked” hint.

1.5.4 · October 23, 2026

  • Fixed: apps opened from a box (such as WPS or a browser) could be grouped under SpringDesk in Task Manager, inflating its reported memory and closing if you ended SpringDesk there. They now open independently as on the Windows desktop.

1.5.3 · October 22, 2026

  • Improved: dragging a selection box inside a box works anywhere outside an icon’s picture and name.
  • Improved: boxes keep the same spacing from screen edges as from each other.
  • Improved: box edges look steadier on busy wallpapers.
  • Improved: SpringDesk uses much less memory while you are not using your boxes.
  • Improved: the Recycle Bin icon in a box updates between empty and full, as on the Windows desktop.
  • Improved: dragging files from a folder box onto the empty desktop moves or copies them following Windows rules.
  • Improved: your layout is kept safe when another program holds its file at start.
  • Fixed: right-clicking an icon could sometimes show no menu on PCs with apps like WPS that add menu items. The menu now opens.
  • Fixed: switching from a picture wallpaper to a solid color could leave boxes showing the old picture, and rolling up a box under the pointer could show a strip of wallpaper across the title bar.
  • Fixed: smaller issues with folder boxes scrolling after a drop, desktop typing opening search right after startup when Use SpringDesk is off, missing icons retrying, and renaming files with repeated extensions when extensions are hidden.

1.5.2 · October 21, 2026

  • Improved: lower memory use.
  • Fixed: box titles and icon names could switch between white and black when boxes moved, rolled up or sat over bright wallpaper patches. All boxes on a screen now share a steady text color with a firmer halo.
  • Fixed: switching from a static to a live wallpaper could leave old wallpaper inside box borders or make borders lag, and rolling up a box could pull up the bottom edge or leave a thin line across the title bar.

1.5.1 · October 21, 2026

  • Fixed: with a live wallpaper on two screens, boxes on one screen could suddenly turn into plain grey panels. They now keep their previous glass picture.
  • Fixed: with a live wallpaper, SpringDesk’s memory use could keep growing over time. Memory now stays steady.

1.5.0 · October 20, 2026

  • New: clear glass look for boxes. At the default 100% transparency, a box is completely clear with no tint or blur, showing only a light-catching rim.
  • New: pointing at a box lights up its edge nearest the pointer and a soft glow follows it.
  • New: rolled-up boxes show only their name on the desktop until you point at them to reveal their glass strip.
  • New: Settings › Appearance now combines Glass and Opacity into one Transparency slider. Lower it to frost the glass for easier reading on busy wallpapers; your existing look is carried over.
  • Improved: box and icon names use white text with a soft dark halo (turning near-black over very light wallpapers) so labels stay readable on any background.

1.4.9 · October 20, 2026

  • Improved: “Show desktop icons” in the desktop’s View menu works as before 1.4.8 and hides boxes when turned off.
  • Improved: quitting SpringDesk restores Windows’ desktop icons at once, even when File Explorer is busy.
  • Improved: items made with New on the desktop still open ready to rename, while files saved or downloaded to the desktop no longer start renaming automatically.
  • Fixed: on some PCs, Windows’ own desktop icons could blink on and off behind the boxes. SpringDesk no longer changes Windows’ “Show desktop icons” setting and restores any change made by 1.4.8.
  • Fixed: files dragged from another computer or a remote session could not be dropped onto the empty desktop. Dropping them works again.

1.4.8 · October 20, 2026

  • New: items created on the desktop with New or Ctrl+Shift+N open in their box ready to rename.
  • New: ticking Windows’ “Show desktop icons” switches SpringDesk off, and unticking it switches SpringDesk on.
  • Improved: switching off or quitting SpringDesk puts Windows’ desktop icons back in their exact places, with new files going where Windows puts new icons.
  • Improved: selection is shared across the whole desktop, as in Windows.
  • Improved: box icons update right away when you change which program opens a file type.
  • Improved: right-click menus show the moment you release the mouse button.
  • Improved: the tray menu matches the boxes’ menus.
  • Improved: smaller download.
  • Fixed: SpringDesk could freeze when File Explorer restarted or get stuck when quitting with a menu open. Both now stay responsive.

1.4.7 · October 19, 2026

  • New: Settings › General › Desktop now has a single Use SpringDesk switch (also in the tray menu) replacing the two earlier desktop icon switches. Turn it off to bring back Windows’ own desktop without quitting while keeping your boxes and layout ready to turn back on.
  • Fixed: documents opened from a box in single-window apps such as WPS could stay behind other windows. They now come to the front.

1.4.6 · October 19, 2026

  • New: inside a box, Shift+arrow keys select multiple icons, Ctrl+Shift+N makes a new folder, and Alt+Enter opens Properties.
  • New: during the free trial, Settings › About shows remaining trial days and a Buy button.
  • Improved: dragging files into and out of boxes follows Windows rules: within a drive they move, and to or from another drive, a USB stick or a network folder they are copied (Ctrl copies, Shift moves).
  • Improved: search supports full pinyin for Chinese names and keeps what you typed if you switch to another window.
  • Improved: downloads in progress wait quietly in the Inbox.
  • Improved: shortcuts to network folders no longer slow SpringDesk down when you are off the network.
  • Fixed: on some PCs, This PC and other system icons never appeared in a box. SpringDesk now shows all enabled Windows system icons and updates right away when desktop icon settings change.
  • Fixed: pressing Ctrl+A, Delete or Enter after clicking the empty desktop could act on hidden Windows desktop icons, and ending SpringDesk in Task Manager could leave icons hidden. Shortcuts no longer affect hidden icons, and desktop icons return immediately.

1.4.5 · October 19, 2026

  • New: press Ctrl+Z in a box to undo your last file operation (move, rename, delete…), just as on the Windows desktop.
  • Improved: dropping files into a box from other programs follows Windows desktop rules (including copy-only drags) and keeps them in the box you drop them on.
  • Improved: keyboard focus stays in the box after renaming, so the next key works there.
  • Improved: restoring a backup tells you which day’s layout was used.
  • Fixed: on some PCs, the Microsoft Store version failed to save boxes, causing them to revert to an older layout after a restart or update. Your boxes are saved now, and the first start of this version brings them back as you last left them.

1.4.4 · October 19, 2026

  • Improved: renaming a file in the sorting box to another type moves it to the matching tab.
  • Improved: dragging a file out of a box onto the empty desktop sends it to the Inbox with a brief flash so you can see where it went.
  • Improved: renaming shows the full name, however long, as on the Windows desktop.
  • Improved: a damaged layout comes back from the latest daily backup.
  • Improved: quitting SpringDesk restores Windows’ desktop icons first so the desktop is never left empty.
  • Improved: diagnostics omit other apps’ window titles and leave your user name out of file paths.
  • Fixed: choosing New from the empty desktop’s right-click menu could show “File System Error (16389)”. Creating new files works again.
  • Fixed: new spreadsheets and presentations made with New (WPS) did not move to their tab in the sorting box, and new files sharing a deleted file’s name were treated as old. Both now sort properly.
  • Fixed: smaller issues with case-only renames, downloads arriving during a drag, and previews of pictures and zip files.

1.4.3 · October 18, 2026

  • New: after a few days of use, SpringDesk asks once whether you would like to rate it in the Microsoft Store, and you can also rate it anytime in Settings › About.

1.4.2 · October 18, 2026

  • Improved: copying and pasting a file into the same tab creates “name - Copy”, as in File Explorer.
  • Fixed: dragging across a file name while renaming picked up the entire icon instead of selecting text. Dragging now selects text.

1.4.1 · October 18, 2026

  • New: frosted glass in Settings › Appearance › Glass melts the wallpaper behind each box into soft, bright color so a busy wallpaper no longer shows through. It follows live wallpapers too.
  • New: resize boxes from their corners as well as their edges, and shrink boxes down to 2 icons wide and 1 row tall.

1.4.0 · October 17, 2026

  • New: first release in the Microsoft Store, with a 7-day free trial.
