How to keep desktop icons clean over Wallpaper Engine on Windows 11

Nothing ruins a great animated wallpaper faster than forty scattered shortcuts, PDFs and screenshots sitting across the middle of the art. Here's how to keep your Wallpaper Engine setup looking clean on Windows 11 without losing quick access to your files.

By Brock Loong, developer of SpringDesk · November 25, 2026

1. Built into Windows 11: hide desktop icons and the taskbar

If you don't use your desktop for files at all, Windows 11 can clear the screen completely in two clicks:

  • Hide all desktop icons: Right-click an empty spot on the desktop and untick View › Show desktop icons. Your Desktop folder still exists in File Explorer; Windows simply stops drawing the icons.
  • Auto-hide the taskbar: Open Settings › Personalization › Taskbar › Taskbar behaviors and tick Automatically hide the taskbar so your live wallpaper reaches all four edges of the monitor.

The catch: the moment you actually want shortcuts, project folders or recent downloads within reach, toggling Show desktop icons through a menu gets old fast, and turning it back on brings all the clutter right back.

2. Clear glass boxes, double-click hide, and auto-fade when idle

I use Wallpaper Engine on my own multi-monitor PC, which is a big reason I designed SpringDesk the way it works on Windows 11:

  • True clear glass at 100% transparency: Instead of painting opaque colored panels over your wallpaper, SpringDesk leaves the inside of each box completely clear: only a thin glass edge catches the light, so live video and scene wallpapers play right through at their full frame rate. (If a wallpaper is extra bright or busy, you can slide transparency down for soft frosted glass.)
  • Roll boxes up to a single line: Click the arrow on a box's title bar (or press Ctrl+Alt+H) to tuck boxes up to just their title along the edge of the screen.
  • Double-click the desktop for pure wallpaper: Double-click any empty spot on the desktop and every box fades out in 0.2 seconds. Double-click again and they're right back.
  • Fade out automatically when you step away: Turn on Settings › Boxes › Hide boxes › When you’re away (for example after 30 seconds or 1 minute). Whenever you lean back or walk away, your boxes gently fade out so your monitor shows nothing but your live wallpaper, and reappear on your first mouse move or key press.
  • New downloads don't scatter across the art: Any screenshot or PDF saved to the desktop goes straight into its tab inside the sorting box instead of landing over the center of your wallpaper.

3. Free alternatives if you don't mind configuring them

If you're looking for a free desktop organizer to pair with live wallpapers, several open-source and freeware options exist, including Desktop Frames+ and Portals. Try whichever fits how you work.

Questions

Do Wallpaper Engine live wallpapers show through SpringDesk's boxes?

Yes. At the default 100% transparency, the middle of every box is genuinely transparent—Windows renders your animated wallpaper directly behind the icons, framed only by a subtle glass edge.

Can I hide all icons automatically when I'm not using the mouse?

Yes. Enable When you’re away under Settings › Boxes › Hide boxes and pick a delay from 10 seconds to 10 minutes. Every box fades out when the PC is idle and returns immediately when you move the mouse or press a key.

What happens if I close or uninstall SpringDesk?

Your files stay untouched in your Desktop folder, and Windows' own desktop icons return to where they were. See how to organize your Windows 11 desktop or how SpringDesk compares with Fences.
