# SpringDesk privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

SpringDesk is a desktop organizer for Windows 11. It works entirely on your PC.

## What SpringDesk collects

Nothing. SpringDesk has no account, no ads, no analytics and no telemetry. It doesn't collect, sell or share
personal data.

## What stays on your PC

- To show your desktop in boxes, SpringDesk reads the names and icons of the items on your desktop, and the contents
  of folders you choose to show in a folder box. It looks inside a file only to show its thumbnail, or a preview when
  you press Space, and it never sends any of it anywhere.
- With frosted glass on (Settings > Appearance), SpringDesk copies the desktop background (the wallpaper, not your
  windows) into memory to draw a blurred version of it behind the boxes. The copy is never saved or sent.
- Your layout (boxes, tabs, settings) and a few daily backups of it are saved in your user folder
  (%APPDATA%\SpringDesk). Log files for troubleshooting are saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SpringDesk.

## Internet

- The version installed from the Microsoft Store gets its updates through the Store and doesn't connect to the
  internet itself.
- The version downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub for new versions from time to time. GitHub receives the usual
  information of a web request, such as your IP address, under GitHub's own privacy statement. SpringDesk sends
  nothing else.

## Diagnostics

Settings > About > Export diagnostics makes a zip file on your PC with the logs, your layout, the names of the items
on your desktop (not their contents) and basic system information (Windows version, screens). It's only shared if
you send it yourself.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/lchovo/SpringDesk.

# SpringDesk 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 18 日

SpringDesk 是 Windows 11 的桌面整理工具，完全在你的电脑上运行。

## SpringDesk 收集什么

什么都不收集。SpringDesk 没有账户、没有广告、没有统计和遥测，不收集、出售或分享任何个人数据。

## 留在你电脑上的

- 为了把桌面显示在分区里，SpringDesk 会读取桌面上各项的名称和图标，以及你选择在文件夹门户里显示的文件夹的内容。只有在显示缩略图、或你按空格预览时，才会读取文件的内容，也从不把任何东西发出去。
- 打开雾面玻璃时（设置 › 外观），SpringDesk 会把桌面背景（壁纸，不包括你的窗口）复制到内存里，在分区后面画出模糊的效果。这份复制不保存、不发送。
- 你的布局（分区、标签页、设置）和每天的几份备份保存在你的用户文件夹里（%APPDATA%\SpringDesk）。用于排查问题的日志保存在 %LOCALAPPDATA%\SpringDesk。

## 联网

- 从微软商店安装的版本通过商店更新，自己不联网。
- 从 GitHub 下载的版本会不时到 GitHub 检查有没有新版本。GitHub 会收到普通网页请求都有的信息（比如 IP 地址），适用 GitHub 自己的隐私声明。SpringDesk 不发送其他任何东西。

## 诊断信息

设置 › 关于 › 导出诊断信息会在你的电脑上生成一个压缩包，里面有日志、你的布局、桌面上各项的名称（不含内容）和基本的系统信息（Windows 版本、屏幕）。只有你自己发出去，别人才会看到。

## 联系

关于隐私的问题：在 github.com/lchovo/SpringDesk 提一个 issue。
