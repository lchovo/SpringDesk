Boxes that stay out of your way

Your icons live in translucent boxes right on the wallpaper, below every window. Drag an icon in and the others make room. Boxes snap to a grid and never overlap, so the desktop stays neat however you move things around.

Tabs, and boxes that roll up

Drop one box onto another to keep more in one place, and switch with a click. Roll a box up to just its title bar when you need the space, and lock your layout so nothing moves by accident.

New files sort themselves

Save or download a file to the desktop and it goes straight to its tab: documents, spreadsheets, slides, PDFs, pictures and more. Anything SpringDesk can't place waits in the Inbox, so nothing goes missing.

Press Space to look inside

Pictures, videos, music, text, code and PDFs open in an instant, without starting another app. The arrow keys flip through the rest. Other files show their details at a glance.

Just start typing

Click the desktop and type, or press Ctrl+Alt+Space from anywhere. Search finds what's in every box and tab, including the folders you show on your desktop.

Light or dark, clear glass

SpringDesk follows Windows' light and dark mode. Boxes are clear glass: only their edges catch the light, and names stay readable on any wallpaper. Lower the transparency and the glass frosts over. It follows live wallpapers too.

Works like Windows

Double-click to open, the usual right-click menu, drag and drop, cut, copy, paste, F2 to rename.

Never loses your layout

A separate layout for each resolution and scaling, and a daily backup you can go back to.

Your files stay put

SpringDesk only shows your desktop in order. Nothing is moved or deleted.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no data collected. It all happens on your PC.

Questions

How much is it?

Try SpringDesk free for 7 days. After that it's a one-time purchase in the Microsoft Store, with updates included and no subscription. Until January 1, 2027 it's US$4.99 instead of US$7.99; the Store shows the price in your currency.

Does SpringDesk move my files?

No. Your files stay in your Desktop folder; SpringDesk only shows them in boxes. Uninstall it and your desktop is exactly as it was.

Does it work on Windows 10?

No, SpringDesk needs Windows 11.

How is it different from Fences?

Fences is the best-known desktop organizer and has more options, such as custom sorting rules and colored fences, and it runs on Windows 10 too. SpringDesk is simpler and costs less: a one-time purchase that is cheaper than one year of the Fences subscription, new files sorted by type without setting up rules, Space to preview files, and typing on the desktop to search. See the full comparison.

I use Fences or another desktop organizer. Can I switch?

Yes. Quit the other organizer first (SpringDesk asks you to if Fences is running), then start SpringDesk: it sorts your desktop into boxes on its first run, and you can rearrange everything from there. Layouts from other apps aren't imported.

Does it collect my data?

No. There's no account, no ads and no data collection; everything happens on your PC. Read the privacy policy.

Is it in my language?

SpringDesk is in English and Chinese, and follows your Windows display language.

Something isn't working.

Open Settings › About › Export diagnostics and open an issue with the zip, or email it to SpringDesk@outlook.com. It holds logs and your layout, with file names but no file contents.
